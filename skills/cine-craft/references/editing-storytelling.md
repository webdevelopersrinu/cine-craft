# Editing & Storytelling

A human editor builds a visual story; they never just concatenate generated
clips. Editing decisions come from the story beat, never from a transition
template.

## Cut vocabulary

| Technique | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Continuity editing | Invisible cuts; space/time stay coherent between shots |
| Cut on action | Cut mid-movement (door opens shot A → finishes in shot B); hides the cut |
| Match cut | Visually/thematically similar frames connect two moments |
| Jump cut | Deliberate time skip within the same framing; energy, vlogs, montage |
| J-cut | Next shot's audio starts before its picture; pulls viewer forward |
| L-cut | Previous shot's audio continues over the next picture; reaction shots |
| Shot-reverse-shot | Conversation coverage, eyelines consistent |
| Cross dissolve | Passage of time or soft mood shift — *occasionally*, never as the house transition |
| Montage | Compress time/meaning through a series; needs a progression, not random beauty |

**Pacing:** clip length varies with the beat — a 0.5s insert next to a 6s
performance hold. Uniform 3-second clips on a metronome is the AI tell. Action
cuts fast; emotion holds long. In music-driven edits, cut on the beat but
break the pattern at the emotional peak.

## Audio design (half the realism)

- Ambience in every shot: room tone, street, wind. Silence reads broken.
- Dialogue leads with J/L-cuts; music ducks under speech.
- Sound effects sync to on-screen actions (footsteps, door, cup set down).
- Music choice follows mood; it ends or shifts with the story beat, not abruptly at the clip boundary.

## The base story spine (default, not mandatory)

1. **Establishing** — the world and where we are.
2. **Character introduction** — who this is about.
3. **Action** — what is happening.
4. **Emotional moment** — the feeling, conflict, or turn.
5. **Resolution** — complete the thought.

Even a 15-second video carries a compressed version of this.

## Per-type grammar

- **Cinematic / film:** the spine above; dramatic motivated lighting, deliberate camera, CU for the turn, longest holds. Grade for mood.
- **Documentary:** handheld/static observational camera, natural light, real locations with life in them, J/L-cuts over interview audio, imperfection is the aesthetic.
- **Commercial:** product is the protagonist. Controlled high-key light, clean composition, macro/ECU product shots, brand-story spine (problem → product in use → payoff → end card). Polished but still physically plausible.
- **Travel / lifestyle:** wide establishing shots, movement motivated by exploration (tracking, walking POV), golden hour, changing perspectives, montage with a geographic or day-arc progression.
- **Interview / talking head:** MCU/CU three-point lit, eyes near camera axis, natural conversational behaviour (see human-behavior.md), b-roll covering jump cuts with L-cut audio.
- **Music video:** edit to the rhythm — cut on beats, double-time at the chorus; expressive lighting and composition allowed to break realism rules *deliberately and consistently*, as style.

## Review pass

Watch the assembled edit asking one question per cut: *what does this cut do
for the story?* A cut with no answer gets removed or replaced. A transition
used more than twice in a row gets justified or varied.
