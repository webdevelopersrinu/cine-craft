# Cinematography — Shots, Movement, Lenses

## Shot sizes (and when a filmmaker reaches for each)

| Shot | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Extreme Wide (EWS) | Scale, isolation, establishing a world. Subject tiny or absent. |
| Wide (WS) | Establish location + subject's place in it. Opens most scenes. |
| Full Shot (FS) | Whole body — physicality, costume, action, dance. |
| Medium (MS) | Waist-up. Conversation default, gestures visible. |
| Medium Close-Up (MCU) | Chest-up. Dialogue with emotional weight building. |
| Close-Up (CU) | Face. The emotional beat. Earn it — don't open on it. |
| Extreme Close-Up (ECU) | Eyes, hands, a detail that carries meaning (ring, trigger, tear). |
| Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) | Conversation geography; connects two people in space. |
| Point of View (POV) | Put the viewer inside the character's perception. |

**Rule:** shot size tracks emotional intensity. A scene typically *moves
inward* (wide → medium → close) as stakes rise. An emotional conversation
lives in MCU/CU; a travel scene lives in WS/EWS. Never one size for everything.

## Camera movements (every move needs a reason)

| Movement | Purpose — the only valid reasons to use it |
|---|---|
| Static | Let performance breathe; stability, observation. The default. |
| Pan | Rotate horizontally — follow a subject, connect two points in space. |
| Tilt | Rotate vertically — reveal height, follow rise/fall. |
| Dolly | Physically move in/out — push in on a realization, pull back to isolate. |
| Tracking | Travel with a subject — walking conversation, chase. |
| Crane / Jib | Vertical travel through space — grand reveal, closing ascent. |
| Handheld | Documentary nerves, immediacy, instability. Subtle sway, not shake. |
| Orbit | Circle a subject — a moment of transformation or a hero product. 1× per video max. |
| Zoom | Focal-length change — rare; reads as archival/doc or deliberate style. |

**AI tells to exclude explicitly:** constant slow push-in on every shot,
aimless floating drift, perfectly smooth gimbal glide on a documentary
subject, orbiting everything. Prompt "locked-off tripod shot" or "subtle
handheld" when stillness is wanted — AI defaults to drift.

## Lens feel (describe it in prompts; it sets perspective and realism)

- **Wide (18–24mm):** environments, interiors, energy; close subjects distort.
- **Normal (35–50mm):** human-eye perspective; documentary and dialogue default.
- **Long (85–135mm):** compressed background, flattering faces, isolates subject with shallow depth of field.
- **Depth of field:** shallow for emotion/separation, deep for geography. Not everything at f/1.4 — all-blurry-background everywhere is a tell.
- **Motion blur:** natural 180° shutter look; moving objects should blur slightly. Frozen crisp motion reads synthetic.
- Real lenses have character: mild edge falloff, believable flares only when a source is in frame.

## Coverage pattern for a conversation

Master WS → OTS on A → OTS on B → MCU singles → CU for the turning line.
Shot-reverse-shot with consistent eyelines (A looks frame-right, B looks
frame-left, always). Crossing the 180° line mid-scene is a continuity error.
